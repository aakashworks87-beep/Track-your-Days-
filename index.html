Phone notifications

Step 1, tap Allow notifications, then keep this app open or in the background on your phone for in app reminders.
Step 2, for a real notification when the app is closed, tap Export below, open the downloaded file, and add it to Google Calendar on your phone. Google Calendar will then remind you, even when this app is closed.

Add to your home screen in Brave

Brave on Android: tap the 3 dots at the bottom right, then Add to Home screen. If Brave shows Install app, tap that instead, it opens full screen like a real app.
Brave on PC: tap the 3 lines at the top right, then Save and share, then Install page as app.
For notifications in Brave: first tap Allow notifications above, then in Brave go to Settings, Site settings, Notifications, and allow this site. On Android also go to phone Settings, Apps, Brave, Notifications, and keep it ON. If a reminder does not show, tap the lion icon in Brave for this site and check Shields are not blocking notifications.
